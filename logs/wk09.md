## Mini Project 3 ideas

Mini Project 3 is an API-powered app. These are brainstorming ideas, not a final choice.

1. Fishing weather checker: look up the weather at a fishing spot to help plan a trip.
2. Exercise finder: look up exercises by muscle group and available equipment.
3. Startup news finder: look up recent news about a company or industry.
