## Something new I picked up

From reading the AI-generated `index.html` and `hello.html`:

- `<!DOCTYPE html>` is not visible on the page. It tells the browser to treat the file as modern HTML.
- `<head>` is setup. `<body>` is what you see. `hello.html` made that obvious because the head is tiny and the body is the heading, nav, list, and footer.
- `charset="UTF-8"` maps bytes to letters so punctuation and emoji do not turn into garbage.
- The viewport `meta` tag is for phones. Without it, a phone pretends the page is a tiny desktop site.
- `href` is where a link goes. `src` is where an image lives. The clickable words sit between `<a>` and `</a>`.
- `<img>` and `<meta>` do not wrap text, so they have no closing tag. `<ul>` is the whole list. Each `<li>` is one bullet.
- `<nav>`, `<section>`, and `<footer>` are labeled boxes. I changed the interests list to my own three items so I was not leaving the agent's placeholder text in the file.
